    Specifications

    What goes into every home

    Structure & walls

    R.C.C. framed structure. Outer walls 9" and inner walls 4½" thick, in lightweight red bricks with cement mortar.

    Doors & windows

    Specially designed polished teak wood main door with exotic hardware. Bedroom doors with medium teak frames and flush shutters; WPC shutters for toilets. UPVC windows with safety grills and mosquito mesh.

    Flooring & plastering

    2'×4' vitrified tiles. Smooth finish on all internal walls; sponge-finish external plaster.

    Kitchen

    Polished granite platform with stainless steel sink and 4' glazed-tile dado above it. Provision for Aqua Guard, exhaust fan and grinder.

    Toilets

    Ceramic glazed tile dado up to 7'. Hindware / CERA or equivalent sanitary ware with branded CP fittings.

    Electrical

    Concealed Havells / V-Guard / Gold Medal or equivalent copper wiring with modular switches. Light, fan and TV points in living and bedrooms, plus provision for fridge, washing machine, mixer, geyser and inverter.

    Painting

    Luppam / putty finish with OBD paint inside. Texture or cement-based paint outside. Polished main door; enamel paint for other doors and windows.

    Plumbing

    Concealed CPVC piping with good-quality fittings. Overhead tank supply with a drinking-water connection in the kitchen, supplemented by a borewell.

    Registration charges, GST and other government levies are borne by the buyer. Changes to a flat can be made on prior request at additional cost; cement racks, arches etc. are optional extras.
